# Exact retained closure, memory, and the completed spin source

This Codex continuation develops the Chapter II retained-state question from
the completed `voli_chapter2_bridge1/MEMORY_DERIVATION.md`. It reuses the original
finite Gibbs source, the original random-site heatbath update, its exchange-odd
sector, all54 completed preparations, and the original `A=N(I-P_transition)`.
No new source integral, fit, preparation, or attempt clock is introduced.
The retained projection `Pi` and transition `P_transition` are different maps.

## 1. Exact closure is equivalent to an invariant retained span

Let `A=A*>=0` on a finite Hilbert space, `V=range(Pi)`, `Q=I-Pi`, and put

    B=Pi A Pi|V, C=Q A Pi|V, D=Q A Q|range(Q).

The block generator is `[[B,C*],[C,D]]`. Its exact retained equation for
`u'=-Au`, `p=Pi u`, `q0=Q u(0)` is

    p'(t)=-B p(t)-C* exp(-Dt)q0
          +integral_0^t K(t-s)p(s) ds,
    K(t)=C* exp(-Dt) C.                                  (M2.1)

The following conditions are equivalent:

1. Every preparation has `p'(t)=-B p(t)` with no hidden initial-state term.
2. `C=0`.
3. `AV` is contained in `V` (and, by self-adjointness, `V` reduces `A`).
4. `Pi exp(-At)|V=exp(-Bt)` for every nonnegative `t`, even if attention is
   restricted to initially retained preparations `q0=0`.
5. `K(0)=0`.

For1->2, the derivative at0 for arbitrary `q0` gives `C*q0=0`. Conditions2
and3 agree by definition; self-adjointness eliminates both cross blocks.
This block decomposition gives1 and4. Conversely, the second derivative in4
gives `Pi A^2 Pi=B^2`, while direct multiplication gives

    Pi A^2 Pi=B^2+C*C.                                   (M2.2)

Thus `C*C=0`, equivalent to2 and5. This includes initially retained states:
setting the hidden preparation to zero removes the forcing term, but does
not remove the memory generated by subsequent excursions.

These are exact linear closure statements in the original Gibbs Hilbert
space. They do not assign new probabilities to signed basis coordinates.
A particular source vector or receiver can annihilate a cross block while
the entire retained account still has nonzero memory.

## 2. The minimal exact enlargement is a source Krylov span

For an ambient dimension `n`, define

    V_min=span{A^j v : v in V, 0<=j<n}.                   (M2.3)

The finite characteristic identity makes `V_min` invariant. Every invariant
space containing `V` contains every displayed vector, so it contains `V_min`.
This identifies the unique smallest exact invariant enlargement. Equivalently,

    V_min=V + span{D^j C v : v in V, j>=0},               (M2.4)

where the sum is orthogonal between `V` and its complementary part. To check
this, the right-hand space is invariant under both generator blocks and
contains `V`; conversely its first complementary block is `CV=QAV`, and
successive applications of `D` lie in the invariant Krylov span.

In particular the first enlargement has dimension

    dim(V+AV)=dim(V)+rank(C).                             (M2.5)

Its dimension is a lower bound for every exact closure. It is not an
assertion that the first enlargement is already invariant.

## 3. A residual is the initial memory strength

For a retained source function `v`, the original projection residual is
`r_v=(I-Pi)Av=Cv`. Therefore

    <v,K(0)v>=||r_v||^2.                                 (M2.6)

If `F(t)=Pi exp(-At)|V`, expansion of the finite semigroup gives

    F(t)-exp(-Bt)=(t^2/2) K(0)+O(t^3).                   (M2.7)

The first derivative is matched exactly by the retained generator. Its
first missed return is second order, measured by the complete residual Gram.
In a nonorthonormal source basis with Gram `G`, Dirichlet matrix `D_b`, and
squared-action matrix `T`, this bilinear Gram is

    L=T-D_b G^-1 D_b,
    [K(0)]_coordinate=G^-1 L.                            (M2.8)

`L` is a bilinear form; its ordinary matrix eigenvalues are not generally
the operator eigenvalues in the source metric. The physical norm uses `G`.

## 4. Kernel, defect, and resolvent bounds from retained source bounds

Suppose `gamma I<=A<=Lambda I` on an invariant sector containing `V`.
Compression gives the same bounds for `B,D`. Spectral calculus gives

    exp(-Lambda t)K(0) <= K(t) <= exp(-gamma t)K(0),        (M2.9)

in the ordering of quadratic forms. More generally
`(-1)^m K^(m)(t)=C* D^m exp(-Dt) C>=0` for every integer `m>=0`.
Memory is an entire family of source-weighted relaxation moments.

The exact initially retained propagator satisfies

    F(t)-exp(-Bt)
      =integral_0^t integral_0^s
        exp[-B(t-s)] C* exp[-D(s-r)] C F(r) dr ds.

It follows that

    ||F(t)-exp(-Bt)|| <= (t^2/2)exp(-gamma t)||K(0)||.     (M2.10)

This is an all-time operator norm bound, not an ordering of the two
propagators. Replacing all three semigroup factors by their identities
also yields the explicit local remainder bound

    ||F(t)-exp(-Bt)-(t^2/2)K(0)||
      <= (Lambda t^3/2)||K(0)||.                         (M2.11)

For the Laplace variable `z>0`, the same account is

    Sigma(z)=C*(z+D)^-1 C,
    Pi(z+A)^-1|V=[z+B-Sigma(z)]^-1,
    K(0)/(z+Lambda) <= Sigma(z) <= K(0)/(z+gamma).         (M2.12)

The minus sign in the retained Schur denominator and plus sign in the
memory integral belong to the same block equation. Signed physical energy
cross terms from another operator remain part of that energy account.

For the original heatbath source, `P_transition` is an average of Gibbs
conditional-expectation projections. Hence `0<=P_transition<=I` and
`0<=A<=N I`. The completed exchange-odd certificates supply the original
`gamma>0` for each family, covering its three distinct preparations.
For these positive-gamma bounds the ambient Hilbert space is explicitly
restricted to the invariant exchange-odd sector `H_odd`, all five retained
source functions belonging to that sector. Here `Q=I_odd-Pi5` and `D` is
its odd complementary compression. The full even sector contains the
stationary function and is not assigned the positive odd-sector gap.
Thus (M2.9)-(M2.12) use `Lambda=N` without a new spectral calculation.
Time is in original A units; Poissonized evolution has mean `N*t` original
uniform site attempts. The original history parameter has mean `N/xi`
attempts. No seconds assignment occurs here. Large `N` makes the lower
kernel envelope conservative; the norm upper envelope and exact initial
coefficient remain source-bound without that large factor.

## 5. Completed spin contractions give a rank-two initial memory

Use the original source bases

    b4=(d,e,u_d,u_e), b5=(b4,w_d), b6=(b4,w_d,w_e),
    G4=<b4,b4>, D4=<b4,A b4>, H=G4^-1 D4,
    W=<w,w>, J11=<w_d,A w_d>, J21=<w_e,A w_d>,
    F11=||A w_d||^2.

The completed identities are `<b4,w>=0` and

    A b4=b4 H+w E^T,
    E=((0,0),(0,0),(1,0),(0,1))^T.

Let `U=< (u_d,u_e),(u_d,u_e) >`, the lower2x2 block of `G4`.
The other2x2 source block is exactly orthogonal to it. Set

    k=(0,0,W11,W21)^T,
    delta=W22-W21^2/W11,
    s=J21-W21*J11/W11,
    R5=F11-k^T G4^-1 k-J11^2/W11.                       (M2.13)

For `Pi5`, the complementary columns of `A b5` vanish on `d,e,u_d`.
The remaining two are

    c_e=w_e-w_d*W21/W11,
    r_next=A w_d-Pi5(A w_d).

Their full Gram is

    L5_nonzero=[[delta,s],[s,R5]],                       (M2.14)

on the source columns `(u_e,w_d)`. In particular
`<w_d,K5(0)w_d>=R5`. The next residual is not the earlier `u_d` or `w_d`.

The completed b6 floor has a different residual:

    R6=||(I-Pi6)A w_d||^2
      =R5-s^2/delta
      =F11-Ffloor(J21).                                 (M2.15)

Thus its retained `Fexcess` is `R6`, not `R5`. The difference is the explicitly
retained payment `s^2/delta`. No even mixed contraction or error interval is
assigned zero when its interval includes zero.

All54 completed source preparations give strict positive enclosures for
`delta`, `R5`, and `delta*R5-s^2`. The two residual directions are independent.
Consequently `rank(C5)=2`, and every invariant extension containing `b5` has
dimension at least7. Its first Krylov enlargement is explicitly

    span(b5,w_e,r_next), dimension7.                    (M2.16)

The completed six-coordinate span `b6` alone remains noninvariant because
`R6>0` in all54 preparations. The next source scalar `<r_next,A r_next>`
would support the selected variational extension; it is not needed for
this exact initial-memory result and is not evaluated here. Calculating
an entire seven-dimensional evolution would require its complete action
blocks, rather than assigning that scalar or other new contractions.
The six-dimensional variational extension `span(b5,r_next)` is different
from the source-floor span called `b6=(b5,w_e)` above. Each adds one of the
two independent complementary directions; neither supplies exact closure
of the full completed b5 account.

Across all54 preparations the independent exact rational intervals satisfy

    1.420936 < R6 < 1.421320,

The coefficient in (M2.7), evaluated on the normalized retained `w_d`, is

## 6. Phase transport preserves the same memory

For a monotone actual phase `theta` with `dtheta/dt=nu(t)>0`, retain
`t=t(theta)`, multiply the entire retained equation by `1/nu(t(theta))`,
and replace each history element `ds` by `dvartheta/nu(t(vartheta))`.
The kernel remains `K(t(theta)-t(vartheta))`. These are separate outside
and history clock factors. A raw phase-difference kernel would specify
another receiving evolution.
