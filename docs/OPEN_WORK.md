# What remains open after this edition

The chapter has executable mathematical results and concrete receiving
protocols. Its status is a development edition with the following explicit
next targets. Later Chapter III work is maintained separately; this list
states what the frozen Chapter II reader itself leaves available.

## Actual minimum spin closure

Continue the literal full-source Krylov construction until exact rank
stabilizes or a source minimal-polynomial identity supplies closure. Current
certificates give a seven-span residual rank of two in all 54 preparations,
lower bounds of ten everywhere and fourteen in the selected deeper case, and
a constructive finite invariant upper space. None identifies the minimum.
The next global higher residual-moment integrals also remain uncomputed here.

## Shared physical calibration and source access

Supply one physical epsilon/time chart, the common source parameter, a ruler
where spatial incidence is used, and preparation/readout access to the named
coherent sectors. The source law links its predictions before each lane is
independently fitted. Endpoint cyclicity and the identified interior blind-mode
exception give algebraic access information; they do not construct a pulse
sequence with measured fidelity.

## Realize the receiving protocols

The paid reference specifies its Hamiltonian, original durations, phase turns
and continuous/switching work. A material implementation must supply its
source actuator, control, preparation, measurement, reset and energy recovery
account. A source wait keeps the reference Hamiltonian active. Signed zero net
work cannot be promoted to zero apparatus cost.

The finite-sample test specifies its detector spectrum, systematic error budget,
timing and sufficient measurement count. The apparatus must establish those
bounds, conditional state quality, independence and calibration confidence.
More efficient receivers and statistical designs can improve the conservative
count; eight theoretical expectations alone are not eight experimental shots.

## Complete localized energy and its external perturbation

Localize source, field, interaction, counterterms, supports and reference under
one common energy convention. Require the integrated account to recover the
complete retained Hamiltonian, and derive the external perturbation from that
same receiving action. A positive global form or selected excitation energy
does not by itself fix a positive local density or the complete gravitational
source. Boundary/reference work remains part of this construction.

Put the derived perturbation into the actual joint-state phase response,
including preparation response, nonzero amplitude and the field energy-domain
bounds. Full-H and epsilon-only scaling are calculated controls with different
remainders; a chosen desired redshift does not select the physical coupling.

## Spatial family, dynamics and apparatus precision

The exact radial receiver controls mesh and outer-wall limits separately. A
source-derived growing three-dimensional spatial family still needs locality,
angular response, isotropy, physical stiffness and boundary exits. Static
Poisson and an endpoint lapse do not assign causal propagation, spatial metric
response or a universal clock coupling.

Finally compare the same-history conditional phase signal with the actual
preparation, timing, unwrapping and readout uncertainty of a calibrated
instrument. Shared theoretical history sharpens the mathematical prediction;
it does not cancel apparatus errors.
